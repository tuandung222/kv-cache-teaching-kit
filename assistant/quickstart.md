# Quickstart

## Good entry prompts

- `Teach me KV Cache in teach mode. Assume I know causal self-attention, but I want systems-level rigor.`
- `Quiz me on KV Cache like a PhD qualifier. One question at a time.`
- `Challenge my explanation of why decode can become bandwidth-bound.`
- `Review this memory-footprint estimate for a 32-layer GQA model with 32k context.`
- `Help me prepare for a 60-minute seminar on KV Cache.`

## Good live-teaching uses

- Ask the assistant to generate cold-open questions for the audience.
- Ask it to turn a slide into a more adversarial discussion prompt.
- Ask it to produce one worked example with numbers for a specific model.
- Ask it to diagnose whether a learner is confusing prefill with decode.

## Good rehearsal uses

- Paste one section of your lecture and ask for missing rigor.
- Ask for five likely misconceptions a research audience may still have.
- Ask for one stronger question after every answer to keep the session Socratic.
